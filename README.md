# Generative AI for Climate Data Assimilation: Diffusion Models for Tracking Tipping Points

## Overview
This repository documents my UROP project on the application of generative AI and diffusion models to climate data assimilation and the study of climate tipping points.

## Objectives
- Understand the mathematical foundations of generative AI, diffusion models, and flow matching.
- Explore the role of generative models in climate data assimilation.
- Study approaches for modelling and tracking climate tipping points.

## Progress
Currently studying the theoretical foundations of flow matching and diffusion models.
